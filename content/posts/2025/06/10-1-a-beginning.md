Hello and welcome to my blog.

That was directed as much at me as it is at you. 

I have no idea what I am going to end up writing here. I have no idea if there will be anything worth reading. I don't even know if I want anyone to read it.

I have set two rules.

First, something must be written here at least once every day. This is likely to be mainly just an interesting encounter or a thought that has come into my head, but may end up including longer and more interesting content. We will see.

The second rule is that once a post is up, it stays up. Whatever happens it stays up.

Maybe at some point this blog will get some readers. Maybe it will not. In either case, I am excited to see what unfolds.
